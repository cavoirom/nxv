Confessions of a Long-Distance Sailor

Created 2023-04-23 · Updated 2024-10-26
  • book
  • sailboat

Through the book, you will experience a snapshot of the world in the nineties, from the west coast of the US to the Mediterranean Sea. You will fall in love with the sea worthiness of Selene, a Crealock 31 sailboat that specially designed for blue water sailing and enhanced with the most modern equipments that the time, unlike the bare minimum style of Pino, another sailboat that I follow for a long time. The book opened a new perspective about an independent and sustainable lifestyle.
