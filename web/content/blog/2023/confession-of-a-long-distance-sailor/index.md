Through [the book](https://arachnoid.com/lutusp/sailbook.html), you will
experience a snapshot of the world in the nineties, from the west coast of the
US to the Mediterranean Sea. You will fall in love with the sea worthiness of
Selene, a Crealock 31 sailboat that specially designed for blue water sailing
and enhanced with the most modern equipments that the time, unlike the bare
minimum style of [Pino](https://100r.co/site/pino.html), another sailboat that I
follow for a long time. The book opened a new perspective about an independent
and sustainable lifestyle.
